0·001 — Overview

The Brief.

India has one of the most diverse hair type demographics in the world. The mainstream hair care market has historically been built around one hair type: straight, smooth, and manageable. For millions of Indian women with naturally curly, wavy, or coily hair, that's meant a lifetime of using products never designed for them, then wondering why the results never match the promise on the packaging.

"The problem is not the curl. The problem is the market that never built for it."

◆ What This Project Covers

Layer 1 — Brand Development: Identity, personality, colour palette, product portfolio · Layer 2 — Market Research: Problem identification, audience profiling, competitor analysis · Layer 3 — Campaign Strategy: Concept, hashtag system, content mix, narrative · Layer 4 — Execution Planning: Content calendar, influencer strategy · Layer 5 — Community Building: Engagement, UGC · Layer 6 — Measurement: KPIs, budget

0·002 — The Brand

The Brand.

Bounce N' Curls is a conceptual Indian hair care brand built around one unapologetic belief: curly hair is not a problem to be fixed, it's a personality to be celebrated. In a market dominated by brands that treat frizz as the enemy, Bounce N' Curls takes the opposite stance.

The brand is designed to feel aspirational yet deeply accessible, speaking directly to young Indian women who've spent years using products not made for their hair type. It's Instagram-first, built for shareability.

0·005 — Competition

The Competition.

The Indian curly hair care segment has grown significantly, driven by the global #CurlyGirlMethod movement. The market remains fragmented, with no single dominant Indian brand authentically owning the curly hair identity.

Competitor 01
Mamaearth
Gap: No curl-specific identity, generalist brand with mass reach but zero curl culture
Competitor 02
WOW Skin Science
Gap: Cold and clinical, low Gen Z connection and no Instagram community
Competitor 03
Pilgrim
Gap: No dedicated curl range, misses the curl community entirely
◆ The White Space

No competitor simultaneously owns curl-first identity, Instagram-native experience, and Gen Z relatability in the Indian market. Bounce N' Curls fills exactly this gap.

0·008 — Influencer Strategy

The Influencers.

Why micro and nano? Smaller audiences have deeper relationships with creators: 5–8% engagement vs 1–2% for macro influencers. Curl-specific micro influencers already have exactly the right audience and deliver authentic reach within a startup-level budget.

0·010 — Reflection

What I Learned.

This project pushed me to think far beyond Instagram posts. Building Bounce N' Curls from a brand name to a full campaign strategy taught me that great marketing starts long before the first piece of content gets made. Understanding the audience, identifying the market gap, studying competitors, and building a cohesive brand identity: these are the real foundations.
